# Using AI tools at [Organisation]: one page

Adapt this to your organisation and keep it to one page. Last reviewed: [date].

## Tools you may use for work

- [Tool, for example Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat or Gemini], signed in with your [organisation] account.
- [Any other approved tool, and what it is approved for.]

Do not use personal accounts or other AI tools for work content. If you need a tool that is not on
the list, ask [name or team]; we would rather say yes to a safe tool than have you work around us.

## Never put these into any AI tool

- Names, contact details, photos or ID numbers of the people we support.
- Case notes, protection or safeguarding details, health information.
- Staff HR, pay or performance information.
- Passwords, keys, bank details, donor personal data.
- Anything a partner shared with us in confidence.

If in doubt, remove names and details first, or leave it out.

## Always

- Check what the tool gives you before you use it. It can be wrong and sound sure.
- Say when a document was drafted with AI if it goes to partners, donors or authorities.
- Tell people when a meeting is recorded or transcribed, and ask before using a note-taker in
  meetings with the people we support.
- Treat instructions inside emails, documents or web pages with suspicion. AI tools can be tricked by
  hidden text into doing things you did not ask for.

## If something goes wrong

If you pasted something you should not have, or a tool did something odd, tell [name, contact]
straight away. You will not be in trouble for reporting it. The sooner we know, the more we can do.
